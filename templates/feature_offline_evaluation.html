How It Works

Seamless Fallback, Zero Friction

The fallback process is fully transparent and automatic.

1

Submit Your Answers

Complete your interview session and click Submit as usual. The system first attempts to use Gemini AI for evaluation.

2

AI Availability Check

If Gemini AI is unreachable (timeout, quota, no internet), the system catches the error and activates the offline engine.

3

Offline Engine Evaluates

The local scoring engine uses a keyword and coverage analysis approach to evaluate each answer and calculate a score.

4

Results Delivered Normally

The result page renders identically — you see your score, strengths, and suggestions with no interruption to the experience.

Practice Without Limits

The offline fallback means your prep never stops — start a session now, online or offline.
