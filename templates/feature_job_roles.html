How It Works

Personalized to Your Career Goal

Your role selection shapes everything — from question topics to evaluation criteria.

1

Choose Your Target Role

Select one of 20+ job roles from the dropdown. Each role has a dedicated question bank covering relevant technical and behavioral topics.

2

Set Your Experience Level

Choose from Fresher, Junior, Mid-Level, or Senior. The platform adjusts the difficulty and depth of questions accordingly.

3

Receive Curated Questions

20 role-specific questions are selected from a shuffled pool of 50+, ensuring broad topic coverage and no repetition.

4

Continue with More Rounds

After each round, choose to practice more. The system draws from the remaining pool so you never see the same question twice per session.
