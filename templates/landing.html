About This Project

This AI Interview Preparation Assistant was developed by an Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning student to help students and job seekers prepare for technical interviews in a simple and effective way.

The platform provides role-based interview questions, AI-powered evaluation, offline scoring, and detailed feedback to help users improve their interview performance and confidence.

It is designed with FastAPI, Python, JavaScript, HTML, CSS, and Google Gemini AI while also supporting offline evaluation for uninterrupted practice.
